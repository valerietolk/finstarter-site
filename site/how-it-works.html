Header

We are ready to guide you through this journey from start to finish.
It looks as follows:

  1. 01Weeks 1–2

    Discovery

    We map your processes, your data, and your regulatory perimeter against the foundation. You see exactly what is already there and what needs building.

  2. 02The long stretch

    Configuration, custom build and integration

    The foundation is configured and extended to fit your model, then connected to your custodians, data providers, and existing systems. This is real engineering work, scoped and estimated up front.

  3. 03Before go-live

    Quality assurance and acceptance

    Functional, security, and performance testing, followed by user acceptance with your own team. Nothing goes live until your people have signed it off.

  4. 04Go-live

    Launch

    Migration, production cutover, and team onboarding. Your brand, end to end, in front of your clients.

  5. 05Ongoing

    Support and evolve

    Our engineering team keeps the platform current — security patching, dependency updates, regulatory change — while you grow on it.
